About

This website provides text-to-speech tools that allow users to generate audio from text using available voice models.

The service is designed to provide a simple interface for exploring and generating speech audio.

For questions, support, privacy requests, or other inquiries, please contact the website owner through the contact method provided on the website.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This website respects your privacy. This policy explains what information may be processed when you use the text-to-speech service.

Information you provide

When you use the text-to-speech service, the text you submit, the selected voice, and selected generation options may be sent to the service provider used to generate the requested audio.

Local storage

The website may use your browser's local storage to remember preferences such as selected voices, recent voices, search preferences, text, emotion settings, and generation state. This information is stored in your browser and is not necessarily sent to our servers.

Third-party services

The text-to-speech functionality uses Fish Audio services. Information necessary to process a speech-generation request may be transmitted to that service.

Cookies and tracking

The website does not currently intentionally use its own advertising or analytics cookies. This may change if advertising or analytics services are added in the future.

Generated audio

Audio generated by the service may be temporarily processed or stored while a generation request is being completed. Temporary files may be automatically removed according to the service's technical operation.

Children

This service is not specifically directed to children under 13.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website's functionality, third-party services, or legal requirements change.
